# Requirements Table

## Hospital Bed & ICU Allocation Dashboard

| Req ID | Type | Description | Priority | Acceptance Criteria | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| FR-001 | Functional | The system shall compute triage severity scores from intake vitals and dynamically order the ER bed allocation waitlist. | High | **Pass:** A patient with a higher acuity score is automatically placed ahead of a lower-acuity patient. **Fail:** The waitlist remains strictly FIFO when a critical patient arrives. | Ensures critical patients receive priority based on medical urgency. |
| FR-002 | Functional | The system shall display the real-time status of ER, ward, and ICU beds as Available, Occupied, or Cleaning. | High | **Pass:** When a bed status changes, the updated status is displayed on the dashboard within 1 second. **Fail:** The dashboard continues showing the previous status after 1 second. | Provides current bed availability for allocation decisions. |
| FR-003 | Functional | The system shall allocate an available ER bed to a patient according to triage priority and bed availability. | High | **Pass:** When an available suitable ER bed exists, the highest-priority waiting patient is assigned to it and the bed changes to Occupied. **Fail:** A lower-priority patient is assigned while a higher-priority eligible patient remains waiting. | Prevents inappropriate allocation and reduces delays for high-acuity patients. |
| FR-004 | Functional | The system shall identify patients requiring ICU transfer and generate an inter-facility transfer request when no suitable ICU bed is available. | High | **Pass:** When an ICU transfer is required and no suitable ICU bed is available, the system creates a transfer request containing patient and destination details. **Fail:** No transfer request is generated under these conditions. | Supports timely escalation when ICU capacity is unavailable. |
| FR-005 | Functional | The system shall send ambulance transfer requests to the ambulance service and record the transfer status. | Medium | **Pass:** A submitted transfer request is sent to the ambulance service and its status changes to Requested, Confirmed, or Rejected based on the response. **Fail:** The request is sent without recording its resulting status. | Enables tracking of inter-facility patient transfers. |
| NFR-001 | Nonfunctional | The system shall propagate bed status transitions (Occupied, Cleaning, Available) to all connected ER dashboard clients in under 1 second via WebSockets. | High | **Pass:** Under simulated peak load, at least 95% of bed-status updates reach connected clients within 1 second. **Fail:** More than 5% of updates exceed 1 second. | Real-time visibility is essential for rapid bed allocation decisions. |
| NFR-002 | Nonfunctional | The system shall restrict patient and bed-management functions to authenticated and authorized users based on their assigned role. | High | **Pass:** Triage Nurses can access triage and bed-allocation functions, Administrators can access management functions, and unauthorized users are denied access. **Fail:** A user can access a function outside their assigned permissions. | Protects patient information and hospital-resource management functions from unauthorized access. |
